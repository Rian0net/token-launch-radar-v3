How the value is derived

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The category multiple is the median market-cap/TVL of the protocols in that category whose token already trades in this snapshot, shrunk toward the global median () with weight n/(n+10) and clamped to [0.04, 1.60]. Growth = 1 + 0.55·tanh(7d change / 30). Maturity = 0.72 + 0.55·(1 − e−age/520d). Breadth = 1 + 0.06·log2(chains), capped at 1.18. The band widens when a category has few launched peers to calibrate on. Protocols that record a token are never ranked — they are the calibration set, listed under “Already launched”.
